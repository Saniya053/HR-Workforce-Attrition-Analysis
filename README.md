# HR Workforce Attrition Analysis

## 1. Problem Statement

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations as it leads to increased recruitment costs, loss of experienced talent, reduced productivity, and disruption of business operations. Understanding the factors that drive employee turnover is essential for developing effective retention strategies.

This project aims to analyze workforce attrition using the IBM HR Attrition dataset and build an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps HR leaders identify attrition trends, understand key drivers of employee turnover, segment high-risk employees, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention.

---

## 2. Dataset Description

The dataset contains employee demographic, professional, and satisfaction-related information used to analyze attrition patterns.

### Dataset Features

| Category             | Attributes                                                         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Employee Information | EmployeeNumber, Age, Gender, MaritalStatus                         |
| Job Information      | Department, JobRole, JobLevel, BusinessTravel                      |
| Compensation         | MonthlyIncome, PercentSalaryHike, StockOptionLevel                 |
| Experience           | TotalWorkingYears, YearsAtCompany, YearsInCurrentRole              |
| Satisfaction Metrics | JobSatisfaction, EnvironmentSatisfaction, RelationshipSatisfaction |
| Work Environment     | WorkLifeBalance, OverTime, TrainingTimesLastYear                   |
| Target Variable      | Attrition                                                          |

### Dataset Size

* Records: 100,000 Employees
* Features: 29 Columns

## 3. KPI Definitions

### Total Employees

Total number of employees in the organization.

### Attrition Count

Number of employees who left the organization.

### Attrition Rate (%)

Percentage of employees who left the organization.

**Formula:**

```text
Attrition Rate = (Attrition Count / Total Employees) × 100
```

### Retention Rate (%)

Percentage of employees retained by the organization.

**Formula:**

```text
Retention Rate = 100 − Attrition Rate
```

### Average Monthly Income

Average salary earned by employees.

### Average Job Satisfaction

Average employee satisfaction score.

### Average Work-Life Balance

Average work-life balance rating across employees.

### Overtime Percentage

Percentage of employees working overtime.

### Average Tenure

Average years employees have spent in the company.

## 5. Key Findings

### Finding 1: Overtime Strongly Influences Attrition

Employees who frequently work overtime exhibit significantly higher attrition rates compared to employees who do not work overtime.

### Finding 2: Low Job Satisfaction Leads to Higher Turnover

Employees with lower job satisfaction scores are more likely to leave the organization.

### Finding 3: Work-Life Balance Impacts Retention

Poor work-life balance is associated with increased employee turnover.

### Finding 4: New Employees Are More Vulnerable

Employees with fewer years at the company are at greater risk of attrition than long-tenured employees.

### Finding 5: Certain Departments Experience Higher Attrition

Departments such as Sales and Research & Development show comparatively higher turnover rates.

### Finding 6: Lower Compensation Increases Attrition Risk

Employees in lower income brackets are more likely to leave the organization.

## 6. Recommendations

### Reduce Employee Overtime

Implement workload balancing strategies and optimize resource allocation to reduce excessive overtime.

### Improve Employee Engagement

Conduct regular employee feedback surveys and introduce recognition programs to improve satisfaction levels.

### Strengthen Work-Life Balance Policies

Promote flexible work arrangements and employee wellness programs.

### Enhance Onboarding Programs

Provide structured onboarding and mentoring support for new employees to improve retention.

### Focus on High-Risk Departments

Develop targeted retention initiatives for departments experiencing higher turnover.

### Career Development Opportunities

Offer training, career progression plans, and skill development programs to improve employee commitment.
